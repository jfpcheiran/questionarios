Título: Beyond the Survey: The Dos and Don'ts of Standardized Questionnaires for Assessing Immersive VR Experiences

Autores: Jean Cheiran, Luciana Nedel, Anderson Maciel, Carla Freitas, Vinicius de Souza

Descrição: As immersive Virtual Reality (VR) expands, accurately measuring User Experience (UX) is critical. While standardized questionnaires offer a reliable alternative to ad hoc surveys, they are frequently misused through inappropriate item modification, poor contextual fit, or unsystematic translations. This tutorial guides evaluators in selecting and administering standardized UX instruments for immersive VR through a comprehensive set of methodological "dos and don'ts" covering conceptual alignment, administration protocols, and structural integrity. Furthermore, it addresses challenges of specific cultural and language contexts. By identifying common pitfalls and providing recommendations, this tutorial empowers the VR community to adopt more reliable measurement strategies, securing the accuracy of empirical research and fostering the development of novel psychometric tools for different contexts of immersive experiences.
